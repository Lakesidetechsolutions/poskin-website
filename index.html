About the mill

Poskin Feed Store is a family-owned feed mill run by Tyler & Marla Litwiller. We carry an extensive Purina line, including Purina's Exclusive pet foods, along with Vita Plus, IDO and FAMO.

When a bag off the shelf isn't quite right, our nutrition staff will help put together a custom feed or mineral mix for your animals.

  • Custom feed for horses, sheep, calves, beef and goats
  • Custom mineral mixes to suit your farm
  • Legend Seeds & Alforex low-lignin alfalfa seed
